What kind of app is it?

Word Hunt is a letter-connection puzzle: swipe through the available letters to discover words and complete a board. The challenge comes from seeing another arrangement in a familiar group of letters, rather than sorting complete word cards into categories or racing another player to claim a grid.

The iPhone and iPad description presents thousands of puzzles, in-game coins earned by completing puzzles and play without timers or a lives limit. Those details make it useful to distinguish this particular Word Hunt from other games with similar names. Here, the listed experience is progressing through word puzzles at your own pace.

The Android description likewise describes connecting letters, progressing through levels and changing scenery, and advertises more than 10,000 puzzles. Counts and offers can differ by platform and version. We do not treat that Android count, or the iOS no-timer and no-lives wording, as proof of identical content and rules on every device.

Is Word Hunt a timed multiplayer word game?

This listing describes a level-based letter-connection puzzle. The iOS description explicitly says there are no timers or lives; it does not describe a head-to-head timed mode.

Can I play offline?

We have not established offline availability from the verified listing facts used for this page. If offline play is essential, check the current description for your device before downloading.
