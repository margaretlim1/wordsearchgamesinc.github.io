One set of letters. Another word to discover.

Look at the letters from a different angle and find the next word. Word Hunt pairs letter-connecting puzzles with a steady sense of progress, awarding in-game coins as you complete boards.

The iPhone and iPad game has thousands of puzzles, with no timers or lives limit. On Android, explore progressive levels and changing scenery, with more than 10,000 puzzles in its store description.

A few things you might wonder.

Is Word Hunt a letter game or a category-card game?

Connect individual letters to make words. For sorting complete word cards into categories, try Solitaire Word Associations.

Can I take my time?

Yes. On iPhone and iPad, puzzles have no timer or lives limit.

Are subscriptions available?

The iOS game offers an optional weekly subscription in supported regions. See the in-app offer for its price and renewal terms.

Where can I find purchase and privacy details?

See the offer in your store for prices, subscriptions and renewal terms. You can read our privacy policy before downloading.
