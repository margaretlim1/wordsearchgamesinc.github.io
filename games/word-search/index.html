Before you play.

Is Word Search free?

Yes. Word Search is a free download on the App Store. In-app purchases may be available; check the store listing for current options.

How do you play Word Search?

Find words by swiping across letters. Keep searching without a countdown. Explore more word puzzles whenever you are ready.

What devices can I play Word Search on?

Word Search runs on iPhone and iPad. Apple devices need iOS 13.0 or later. You can also play Word Search in your browser. The store provides the latest requirements and regional availability.

Who publishes this game?

The App Store lists Word Search Games Inc. as the publisher of Word Search. It was first released on 19 March 2018.
