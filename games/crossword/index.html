Is Crossword free?

Yes. Crossword is a free download on the App Store. In-app purchases may be available; check the store listing for current options.

How do you play Crossword?

Work out missing words from clues. Use hints when a clue has you stuck. Play at your pace, including offline.

What devices can I play Crossword on?

Crossword runs on iPhone and iPad. Apple devices need iOS 13.0 or later. The store provides the latest requirements and regional availability.

Who publishes this game?

The App Store lists Word Search Games Inc. as the publisher of Crossword. It was first released on 20 July 2019.
