MENTAL HEALTH & COGNITIVE-HEALTH RESEARCH

Thoughtful play. A sharper purpose.

We’re working with researchers, PhDs and professors affiliated with the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH) and Wilfrid Laurier University to explore mental health, cognitive engagement and the experience of play.

Our goal is to help people keep their minds sharp and support cognitive health as they age. Through player feedback and research-led exploration, we’re studying questions around attention, memory and everyday wellbeing.

Player-experience research is ongoing. The proposed Laurier project is in development.
